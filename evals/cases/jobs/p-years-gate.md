Senior Product Manager, Payments Platform — Corebank (Bengaluru)

Corebank builds core banking and payments infrastructure for Indian banks. As a Senior Product Manager you will own our payments platform roadmap: UPI, IMPS and settlement.

Responsibilities
- Own the payments platform roadmap and quarterly planning.
- Partner with engineering, risk and finance on new payment rails.
- Run experiments and analyse funnel data to improve success rates.
- Work directly with bank partners and merchants to understand needs.
- Write PRDs and acceptance criteria and drive releases.

About you
- Deep understanding of UPI and Indian payment systems.
- Strong analytical skills; comfortable with SQL.
- Excellent communication with technical and business stakeholders.
- A track record of shipping payment products used at scale.

Requirements
- Minimum 7 years of product management experience is required for this role.
- Bachelor's degree in engineering or a related field.
