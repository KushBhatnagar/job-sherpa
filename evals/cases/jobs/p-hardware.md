Product Manager, Semiconductor Test Equipment — Siliconworks (Bengaluru)

Siliconworks designs automated test equipment for semiconductor fabs. The Product Manager will own our wafer-test hardware line.

Responsibilities
- Define requirements for next-generation wafer probers and test heads.
- Work with hardware, firmware and manufacturing engineering.
- Manage BOM cost targets and supplier roadmaps.
- Support field application engineers at customer fabs.

Requirements
- 5+ years of product management for capital equipment or semiconductor hardware.
- Degree in electrical engineering or physics.
- Understanding of wafer test, probe cards and ATE platforms.
- Willingness to travel to customer fabs in Taiwan and Korea up to 40% of the time.
